## Working method (do not skip)
- **One change at a time, measured**; rebuild → run → keep/revert before the next. Verify every AI/agent claim against the actual code (they overstate).
- **Speed items: gate = byte-identical** (eval/move/PV/nodes) + faster, else revert. **Measure at a FORCED FIXED DEPTH** — the engine straddles a depth-step boundary, so time-bounded totals swing a whole ply and aren't comparable across builds.
- **Strength items: behavioral** (output changes) — judged on the **tactical harness**, NOT byte-identity, at a fixed depth. Watch for **churn**: a *correct* fix that regresses positions reveals the order-sensitivity.

### What we PROVED (the chain)
1. **Eval is SOUND, not the bug.** Built `ChessAI.ev()` + `eval_at_resolution.py` (freeze SF deep-PV resolution FENs once, re-score our *static* eval there). Wherever a static-vs-static comparison is meaningful (WAC.204 ≈ equal, .018 White +10) our static **agrees with Stockfish**. The deep-misevals (engine thinks the winning side is losing, e.g. WAC.213 search ≈ −9 pawns) are **forcing/mating-sacrifice lines** — a static eval can't represent a mate; that −9000 is the SEARCH-backed value. ⇒ **the bug is the search.**
2. **LMR is the SOLE culprit** (ablation via `ENABLE_*` toggles + `ablation_sweep.py`, deep12 @ d10): baseline 3/43; all-pruning-off 32/43; **`no_LMR` alone 31/43 (+28)**; futility/razoring/null-move/q-delta each +0…+3. Mechanism: an LMR-reduced quiet move fails low at reduced depth; the re-search gate (`score>alpha && score<beta`) never fires on the fail-low → the move dies at its reduced value (no extensions exist).
3. **Stockfish ladder** (deep12): SF d10 18 / d12 23 / d15 28 — SF *also* over-prunes at a hard d10 (it needs depth-margin), so "solve @ fixed d10" is a confounded metric; but SF's d10 (18) >> ours (3) ⇒ its cuts are smarter. Real strength = depth-per-second. ~8 of the 43 are genuinely **depth-gated** even full-width (incl. WAC.213).
4. **Profiler killed the easy levers.** `LMR_PROFILE` bucketed 5.8M reductions: dropped moves are uniformly **low history-VALUE** (so "reduce-less-for-high-history" can't fire) and indistinguishable from noise by every local signal ⇒ selection-heuristic LMR is tapped out; **verification (re-search) is the lever.** Dead ends, all measured: `PROTECT_KILLERS` +0 (killers already ordering-boosted), `PROTECT_PV` +7 but **4.3×** (full-window nodes ≈ half the tree; PV move already protected via the live PV ordering bonus at search_engine.cpp:599-605), `DELTA_MARGIN` (q-delta-off = ∞ margin = +0).
