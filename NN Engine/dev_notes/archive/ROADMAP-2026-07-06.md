  pruned (WAC: +9% nodes 39.1M→42.7M, FMC FLAT 87.3→87.2%, +3 solves; corpus EBF 3.47→3.64). The win is those added
  nodes happening to include the refutations. ⇒ the real fix is PRUNE SMARTER (statScore = reduce-less-good AND

  the tree). 87% ≠ ceiling → **move ORDERING has room again**, MOST on quiet/positional positions (corpus ~85%) where the
  FROZEN move-gen cache hurts worst → **REOPENS the ordering lane** (Q8.1 tail re-sort / staged picker). ⚠️ VERIFY vs SF's
