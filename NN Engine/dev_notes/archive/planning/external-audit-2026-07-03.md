## Strategic conclusions (7-question round, distilled)

1. **Eval-accuracy↔strength thesis is COMPLETED, not flipped.** Our "eval accuracy ≠ strength" was overgeneralized from **single additive terms** (low flip-density, +NPS, noise-everywhere → of course they wash). A global retune of existing constants is a different object: **NPS-identical by construction**, gains via the two channels the thesis names (thousands of distributed decision-flips + a lower eval-noise floor that makes every margin-denominated pruning decision safer). **Measure success as `Texel bundle` + `margin re-sweep` COMBINED, never the standalone eval SPRT** (gating on the bundle alone kills a winner at half-payout).
2. **Recalibration vs missing concepts.** Retune **cannot** close king-safety (feature is *absent from the basis*, not mis-weighted → expect only 1-2p partial recovery via KS-correlated terms). **The KS residual after retune is the honest spec for the king-bucketed-PST build.** Retune **can** de-noise the central maps (they're already linear square-weights → cell-recalibration is exactly PST-Texel). Context-dependence (central value × pawn-structure/king-file) needs **bucketed tables**, not nonlinearity — bucketed-linear = conditioning expressed as table structure, fully inside the logistic fit. **Build the fit infra so a table splits into K buckets with no new code** = the upgrade path from recalibrate → interactions without leaving Texel.
3. **Teacher-strength ceiling: corpus caps COVERAGE not CALIBRATION.** Texel learns P(outcome|position); 2700-player weakness enters as label noise absorbed by K. SF-adjudication already launders conversion noise. **Label = `λ·outcome + (1−λ)·SF_WDL`, λ≈0.5** (SF eval → WDL). This is NOT the dead SF-total fit (passes through fitted K; scale confound stays dead) and is **byte-identical to the eventual NNUE data pipeline**. Risk = variance asymmetry (fix λ + source proportions across rounds). Diversify blind spots with a slice of SF-vs-SF / chess.com data.
4. **Order of operations: staged, ONE pass.** (1) retune eval search-frozen → (2) SPRT eval bundle standalone → (3) **re-sweep the margin family** against the new eval (`FUTILITY_MARGINS`, razor thresholds, `VERIFY_MARGIN`, `DELTA`, `LMP_BASE`, `ASPIRATION_DELTA` — all eval-unit-denominated, calibrated to the OLD noise floor) → (4) SPRT again. K-anchored + material-pinned ⇒ scale barely moves ⇒ **old margins become conservative, not unsound** → skipping step 3 *locks up* Elo, doesn't blow up. This is the capg→`LMP_BASE=1` experience as a general law: **cleaner eval is spent by tightening margins; margins don't tighten themselves.**
5. **Conditioning-vs-addition mechanism (3-tier philosophy).** Why additive washes / conditioned ships here: (a) **noise economics** — flat term fires regardless of context, taxing every margin decision where irrelevant; aggressive pruning = high noise tax = unusually high ship bar (why generic "add mobility/outposts" advice keeps failing *for us specifically*); (b) **basis saturation** — after years of hand-tuning the flat basis is near-optimal, a new additive term is a correlated regressor with ~0 marginal coef; what's unexplained is *interactions* = product features; (c) search masks static accuracy except at flips. **Refined philosophy = 3 tiers:** (1) recalibrate globally [Texel — neither add nor condition, untried], (2) interact existing features [buckets/products = systematic conditioning], (3) new detectors ONLY with demonstrated flip-density (pvb_delta/classify_failures).
6. **96% FMC vs "RFP is top lever" — different node populations.** FMC = ordering quality at *cutoff* nodes. RFP's customers = *fail-high nodes that currently pay ≥1 child search to prove it*; RFP takes them from **one child search to zero** when `eval−margin ≥ beta`. Near-leaf shallow nodes dominate exponentially; null-move's gates (`cur_depth≥3/4`, `depth_limit≥5`, material) leave exactly the **shallow-remaining region uncovered** → RFP is the cheap specialist there. **96% FMC does NOT cap it** — binding constraint on RFP aggressiveness is **eval noise** (margin must dominate it), not ordering. Post-retune FMC stays 96% while EBF falls. (Impl: sign-mirror — minimizer side `eval + margin ≤ alpha → return eval`.)
7. **Guardrails + gate.** Regularize **Δw not w** (L2 toward current hand-values — strong battle-tested prior; λ by held-out logloss; this alone prevents most of the detector-knob failure). **Second-difference smoothness** on tables (unregularized PST fits produce checkerboard cells = visible overfit). **Trust region** ≤~25%/round, iterate. **Three holdouts:** game-level split + time split (train old, validate newest — catches drift/leakage) + frozen FEN control the fit never sees. Funnel stays sovereign (fit → benches → SPRT; the −4.9% failure was a fit accepted *without* the gate). **d4 fast-gate valid for THIS bundle** (strategic — capg/KS/latent_threat kept OUT), **one-sided** (pass→lightning; fail→inspect, d4 under-samples endgames), PACE/move-match as zeroth gate.
